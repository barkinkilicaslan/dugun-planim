## Description

```text
Düğün Planım brings your wedding preparations into one calm plan, with no account required. The app is available in Turkish and English; it follows your device language automatically, or you can change it in Settings.

Organize tasks by date, category and priority, spot overdue items and follow your preparation progress. Pick your wedding date from the calendar and the starter tasks are prepared around it.

GUESTS AND RSVP
- Keep guests with name, side, group, number of adults and children, and meal or allergy notes.
- If you like, tap "Add guests from contacts" to bring in only the people you select. The Contacts permission is requested only when you tap that option; if you decline, the rest of the app keeps working.
- Mark replies yourself as Attending, Not attending, Maybe or Awaiting reply. Replies do not arrive in the app automatically; you update them.
- Move your list with CSV import and export.

INVITATIONS
- Choose from 10 ready-made templates: Classic, Minimal, Botanical, Modern, Bohemian, Gold Elegance, Garden, Geometric, Night and Romantic.
- Add a photo if you wish and adjust the text and colors.
- Create your invitation as a PNG or PDF on your device.
- You stay in control of sending: the e-mail, SMS, WhatsApp or share screen opens only when you tap the matching button, and you confirm the send in the system screen. The app never sends a message silently.
- Add an optional "adults only" note to the message.

VENUE AND SEATING PLAN
- Arrange tables, the stage, dance floor, entrance and other areas to match your venue; adjust shape, size and angle.
- Assign guests to tables with capacity checks.
- Share your seating plan as a PDF summary.

BUDGET AND VENDORS
- Track planned, actual and paid amounts by category.
- Keep vendor quotes, contract status and payment schedules.
- See task and payment dates together in the calendar.

PRIVACY
- There is no user account or data server; your planning data stays on your device.
- No advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs are used.
- There is no online RSVP service.
- You choose the destination of JSON backups, CSV and PDF files yourself. Invitation photos are not included in the JSON backup.
- Optional local task reminders are available.

This app does not provide professional wedding, legal or financial advice.
```

## App Review notes

```text
No account or demo login is needed; the app has no user accounts.

First launch: tap "Let's start", enter two names, tap Continue, pick a wedding date from the calendar, enter an estimated budget and guest count, tap Continue, and choose "Skip for now" on the notification screen. The bottom tabs then lead to Home, Tasks, Guests, Budget and More.

Contacts permission: it is requested only when you tap Guests > "Add guests from contacts", after the explanation has been read. If it is denied the rest of the app works and guests can be added manually. Only the name, phone number and e-mail of the contacts the user selects are saved to the guest list on the device; nothing is sent to a server.

Invitations and sending: More > Invitations has 10 templates; PNG/PDF files are created on the device. The e-mail, SMS, WhatsApp and share screens open only when the user taps the matching button, and the user confirms the send in the operating system screen. The app never sends messages silently.

Online RSVP is off; there is no server or backend that collects replies. Guest replies (Attending, Not attending, Maybe, Awaiting reply) are marked manually and stored locally by the user.

Language: Settings > Language lets you choose Automatic, Türkçe or English.

Deleting all data: Settings > "Delete all my data" asks for two confirmations and clears local data and reminders.

Encryption: the app uses no custom (non-standard) encryption; ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is set to false. The app does not send user data to the developer's server and has no advertising or tracking SDKs.
```
