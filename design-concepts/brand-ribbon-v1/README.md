## Approved icon generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished standalone square app icon concept for Düğün Planım, a premium joyful wedding planning mobile app. Full bleed opaque square background, no pre-rounded outer corners, no phone mockup, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no presentation board. Strong original centered silhouette with generous 20% safe margins, crisp flat vector-like graphic design, exceptional clarity at 48px, only two or three solid colors. Sophisticated contemporary visual identity, not clipart, not a detailed illustration, no metallic textures, no 3D bevel, no drop shadows, no flowers or ornamental frame. Concept 2 'Kurdele': solid vibrant coral-pink background #E85D70. One warm ivory broad-stroke abstract ribbon knot, two asymmetric rounded loops above and two short elegantly tapered ends below. Minimal sculptural bow emblem evokes tying the knot and an invitation ribbon. Sophisticated compact graphic geometry, controlled negative space, balanced generous margins, bold and readable, not a gift box, not butterfly wings, not a childish bow.
